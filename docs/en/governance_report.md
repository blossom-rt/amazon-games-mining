## Comparison of Imputation Strategies

The target is product price. From the 11,907 products with price greater than 0, a 20% sample stratified by main category is held out as the test set (2,382 products), and the rest is used for training.

| Strategy | Description | MAE | RMSE | MedAE | R² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global median | Training-set price median, ignoring category | 31.25 | 76.53 | 11.00 | -0.1004 |
| Category median | Median per main_cat_clean group, missing groups fall back to the global median | 29.97 | 74.94 | 11.44 | -0.0552 |
| KNN neighbors | Weighted average of the 5 nearest neighbors after standardization; features include product list length and text length | 40.10 | 83.14 | 17.95 | -0.2987 |
| Iterative regression | IterativeImputer regresses price iteratively on the remaining product features | 40.11 | 83.22 | 17.87 | -0.3010 |

By MAE, the best strategy is the **category median**. But the test set comes from observed prices and cannot represent the missingness mechanism of the 83% unobserved prices; it only shows "within existing prices, which backfill is more stable".

## Outlier Detection Comparison

| Method | Scope | Threshold | Detected | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price IQR | products, price>0 | upper bound 86.97 (lower bound negative, no low-price outliers) | 1,129 | 9.48% |
| Review length IQR | all reviews | upper bound 912 (lower bound negative) | 271,666 | 10.59% |
| Review length z-score | all reviews | \|z\| > 3 (mean 401, standard deviation 811) | 48,758 | 1.90% |
| IsolationForest | 200,000-row sample | contamination=0.01 | 2,000 | 1.00% |

On the same sample, IsolationForest and the length IQR overlap on 1,937 rows. The anomalies the two methods focus on are not fully consistent: IQR looks only at the univariate tail, whereas the isolation forest also takes vote count and image count into account.

## Handling Recommendations

- Apply log1p to review length and word count before they enter the model, and clip at p99 within the training fold; keep the raw values for auditing
- Do not delete prices equal to 0; use price_missing to indicate missingness during modeling, and winsorize observed prices at p1/p99 within the training fold
- Use IsolationForest only for sampled manual review, never to delete rows directly; long reviews may be genuine long text
